## Resume Tips

### Format
- 1-2 pages max
- Reverse chronological order
- Quantifiable achievements (numbers, %, $)
- Action verbs: "Led", "Increased", "Developed"

### ATS Optimization
- Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
- No tables or graphics
- Keywords from job description
- PDF format

## Cover Letter Framework

```
Paragraph 1: Hook + why this company
Paragraph 2: Relevant experience + achievements
Paragraph 3: Why you're a great fit
Paragraph 4: Call to action
```

## Application Tracking

Track for each application:
- Company & position
- Date applied
- Status (Bookmarked → Applied → Interviewing → Offer)
- Next steps & deadlines
- Interview notes

## Interview Preparation

### STAR Method
- **S**ituation - Context
- **T**ask - Your responsibility
- **A**ction - What you did
- **R**esult - Outcome with numbers

### Questions to Ask
- "What does success look like after 90 days?"
- "What challenges is the team facing?"
- "What's the growth path for this role?"

## Salary Negotiation

- Research market rates on [Levels.fyi](https://levels.fyi) and [Glassdoor](https://glassdoor.com/Salaries)
- Don't give the first number
- Always negotiate - most offers have room
- Consider total comp (base + bonus + equity)
